AI & machine learning for copolymers

My PhD research at HKUST focuses on developing AI and machine learning methods for understanding, predicting, and designing copolymers — building on a foundation in AI-enhanced computational chemistry and machine learning interatomic potentials developed during my Master's at Xiamen University under Prof. Pavlo O. Dral.

I'm broadly interested in how machine learning can compress the loop between a research question and a validated molecular or material design — whether that's a copolymer sequence, a reaction pathway, or a potential energy surface.

From copolymers to autonomous chemistry

A quick look at the threads running through my work — see the Research page for the full picture, straight from my CV.

01

AI for Copolymer Design

Current PhD research at HKUST: machine learning methods to predict and design copolymer structure–property relationships.

02

Machine Learning Potentials

Developing MLIPs aimed at surpassing standard DFT accuracy — descriptor engineering, training, and rigorous benchmarking.

03

AI Agents for Science

Fine-tuned domain LLMs (LoRA), RAG knowledge systems, and multi-agent workflows (LangGraph) for autonomous computational chemistry.

04

Topological Materials

First-principles DFT calculations and ML-assisted screening to discover new functional topological materials.

Flagship Project

Aitomia

An agentic, AI-driven platform integrating large language models, scientific workflows, and machine learning tools (MLatom, AIQM) to streamline computational chemistry — from input generation to result interpretation. Published in Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation, 2026.

Hu, J.; Nawaz, H.; Hou, Y. F.; et al. — Hu, Nawaz, and Hou contributed equally as co-first authors.
